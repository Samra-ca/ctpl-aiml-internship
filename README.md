#  About The Internship

This repository contains all learning materials, tasks, assignments, projects, reports, notebooks, and documentation completed during the **8-Week Onsite AI/ML Internship Program** at **Cooperative Technology Company (CTPL)**.

The internship is designed to provide practical industry-level experience in **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analysis, SQL, Statistics, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Generative AI, and AI application deployment**.

Throughout the internship, I worked on real-world datasets (IBM HR Analytics, Breast Cancer, California Housing, UCI Bank Marketing, Telco Customer Churn, CIFAR-10) and built multiple deployment-ready AI applications, including **3 complete end-to-end projects**.

All work completed throughout the internship is organized week-wise and maintained in this repository for learning, documentation, and portfolio development purposes.

#  Projects & Applications

### Project 1 — Employee Attrition Predictor *(Week 3)*
Predicts whether an employee is likely to leave the company using Machine Learning.
- **Dataset:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition (1,470 records)
- **Models:** Logistic Regression, Random Forest
- **Deployment:** Interactive Streamlit dashboard
- [View Folder](week-3/project-1/employee-attrition-predictor/)

### Project 2 — Bank Marketing Model Comparison *(Week 4)*
Complete machine learning workflow to predict term deposit subscription.
- **Dataset:** UCI Bank Marketing (45,211 records, 17 features)
- **Models:** Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, SVM
- **Techniques:** Feature engineering, SMOTE, StandardScaler, Label/One-Hot encoding

### AI Local Business Marketing Agent *(Week 5)*
AI-powered digital marketing assistant for local businesses.
- **Features:** Business profile management, AI-generated marketing content, social/email/WhatsApp campaigns, Google Business integration, sentiment analysis, campaign analytics
- **Repository:** https://github.com/Samra-ca/Business-Marketing-Agent

### PCB Defect Detection System *(Week 7)*
Automated inspection system for printed circuit boards.
- **Model:** CNN, 98.2% test accuracy, 0.98 Macro F1 score
- **Features:** Grad-CAM heatmaps, 6 defect types, batch inspection, repair-vs-scrap logic, yield analytics
- **Stack:** TensorFlow/Keras, FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL
- **Repository:** https://github.com/Samra-ca/PCB-Defect-Detection-System

### CareerMind.AI *(Week 8)*
Full-stack AI-powered career development platform.
- **Features:** AI career chat, skill gap analysis, roadmaps, job recommendations, resume/ATS analysis, LinkedIn auditing, mock interviews, cover letters, salary insights, application tracking, analytics dashboard
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini (primary) + Groq (fallback)
- **Stack:** React/Vite, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Firebase Authentication
- **Repository:** https://github.com/Samra-ca/CareerMind-AI

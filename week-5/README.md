# Week 5 – AI Local Business Marketing Agent

During Week 5 of my AI/ML internship, I developed an **AI Local Business Marketing Agent** designed to help local businesses manage and improve their digital marketing activities.

The project includes features for:
- Business profile management
- AI-generated marketing content
- Social media campaign management
- Email and WhatsApp campaigns
- Google Business integration
- Sentiment analysis
- Campaign analytics and performance tracking
- A user-friendly marketing dashboard

# SQL Queries Summary

## Dataset
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset

## Tasks Performed

1. Displayed all employee records.
2. Filtered employees with Monthly Income greater than 5000.
3. Displayed top 10 highest income employees.
4. Counted employees by department.
5. Calculated average income by department.
6. Found minimum and maximum income by department.
7. Identified employees who left the company.
8. Ranked employees according to Monthly Income.
9. Calculated running totals using window functions.

## Concepts Learned

- SELECT
- WHERE
- ORDER BY
- LIMIT
- GROUP BY
- Aggregate Functions
- Window Functions

## Conclusion

SQL was used to retrieve, filter, aggregate, and analyze employee data efficiently.
